1. Understanding Fabric Textures

  • Stiff: Very firm fabric, perfect for formal events.
  • Half-Stiff: Medium firmness, the most common choice for daily use.
  • Quarter-Stiff: Soft and comfortable with a slight shape.
  • Silky Soft: Very smooth and light on the body.

2. Sizing Information

All our fabric pieces come in a standard length of 3.5 Meters, which is enough to tailor one adult thobe.
